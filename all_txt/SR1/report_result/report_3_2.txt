  "Extracted_Contents": "Collected items included: study characteristics (author, year, title, design, sample size, country); participant characteristics (age, sex, ethnicity, type of pain); intervention/comparator details (opioids prescribed in each arm, pharmacogenomic test/platform used, genes tested); and outcomes measured. Outcomes encompassed pain intensity/severity (e.g., VAS, numeric rating scale, PROMIS, composite pain score), opioid consumption (morphine milligram equivalents), adverse event incidence/frequency (e.g., total adverse events), and symptom severity scales (e.g., MDASI), along with group means/medians, standard deviations or ranges when reported, sample sizes per arm, follow-up time, p values, and (as summarised in tables) prescribing guidelines used (e.g., CPIC, pharmacist recommendations). Additional review-level data included risk of bias judgements using RoB 2 (domain-level and overall) and GRADE certainty ratings per outcome. Overall, the extracted data covered the core elements required for systematic review analyses; however, some trials did not report standard deviations for certain outcomes (limiting meta-analysis inclusion), and adverse events were reported in detail by only one trial."
